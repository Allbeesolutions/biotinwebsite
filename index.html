Important information

Medical disclaimer

Everything on this website is general information about our clinic and the products we stock. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation, and it is no substitute for consulting a qualified doctor. Never delay seeking medical advice because of something you read here. In an emergency, call your nearest hospital immediately.

Prescription medicines

Many items in our catalogue are prescription-only under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. The “Rx” and “OTC” labels shown on product cards are a guide only and may not be accurate for every item. We dispense prescription medicines only against a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner, and our pharmacist confirms every order before it is filled. Adding an item to the cart is a request, not a confirmed sale.

Prices and availability

Prices shown are the printed maximum retail price and may change without notice. Stock levels come from our pharmacy system and can move during the day, so an item shown as available may sell out before your order is confirmed. Our pharmacist will confirm the final price and availability on WhatsApp before dispensing.

Your information

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking, and it does not store anything you type. When you submit the appointment form or a medicine order, the details you entered are placed into a WhatsApp message that you send to us — nothing is transmitted until you press send in WhatsApp. We use those details only to contact you about your appointment or order. To have your details removed from our records, message us on WhatsApp.
